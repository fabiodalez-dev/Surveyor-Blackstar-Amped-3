🎸 Tired of needing a PC to tweak your Blackstar AMPED pedal? 📱🔌

Hey everyone! If you own a Blackstar AMPED (like the Amped 3) and hate having to plug it into a computer just to change CabRig settings or adjust the deep EQ, I've got something for you. 

I’ve spent the last few weeks reverse-engineering the USB protocol and built Surveyor – a 100% free, open-source Android app that gives you full control of your amp directly from your phone using a simple USB-OTG cable! 

What it does right now:
✅ Live Sync: Move a physical knob on the amp, it moves on the app. Move a slider on the app, your amp responds instantly.
✅ Full CabRig Control: Access all hidden 288 CabRig combinations (24 cabs x 6 mics), room levels, and deep EQ cuts without a PC.
✅ Mobile Presets: Import official Architect patches to your phone, edit them, and burn them directly to the hardware slots.

🔥 THE BIG NEWS (The Future):
While reverse-engineering the app, we completely cracked Blackstar’s proprietary CabRig DSP format! We discovered they don't use heavy WAV IRs, but an ultra-low latency architecture (a 16-biquad cascade using 65 float parameters). 
Because we fully decoded this format and its checksums, we are currently experimenting with a tool that uses mathematical curve-fitting to convert ANY standard WAV IR into Blackstar's format. 
Yes, this means true Custom IR support for the Amped series is technically possible and is in the works! 🤯

You don't need Android Studio to use it. Just grab the APK from the GitHub releases and plug in your amp!
We also gladly accept Issues and Pull Requests on GitHub if you find bugs or want to contribute to the DSP reverse-engineering! 🛠️
